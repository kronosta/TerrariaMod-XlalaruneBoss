This mod adds a new boss named XR Primary, based off a fictional species I designed based off an AI model I trained that turned out slightly wrong but in a really cool-looking way.

This boss fight is partially bullet hell but has some weird mechanics that boss fights don't typically use.

Because of these mechanics, until you equip the Fold and Wise accessory, I would suggest you use spare weapons to fight,
lest you get "Guitared".
